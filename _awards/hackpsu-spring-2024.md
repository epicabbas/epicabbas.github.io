Won Best Web Hack Using React at Penn State's student-run hackathon, competing over 24 hours with 17 teams from around the world. With a team of five I helped ideate CommunitySOS and design the front end — an opt-in platform that lets communities mobilize volunteers for urgent local needs that fall outside traditional emergency response.
